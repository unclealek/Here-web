# Pre-launch legal-page review

The `/terms`, `/privacy` and `/community-guidelines` pages are **drafts**, not approved legal documents. They are marked accordingly on the website and set `noindex` to avoid accidental indexing before review.

Before making these final:

- Confirm HERE's legal controller / trading entity, business address, legal contact and support email (do not assume More Data's legal location).
- Confirm product audience, 18+ enforcement, account creation (Google/Apple), content moderation, reporting, blocking, deletion and appeals.
- Audit **actual app** collection and processing: precise/approximate/background location, approximate venue presence, external maps/places, crash analytics, notifications, event hosts/attendees, IP addresses, retention and backups.
- Confirm providers, processors, sub-processors, regions, international data transfers and GDPR lawful bases.
- Confirm the public URL data fields and whether link holders can forward invites, what happens when link is revoked or a gathering ends.
- Confirm retention periods per category and whether presence is actually deleted in 24 hours, rather than relying on design intentions.
- Review consumer law, content license, acceptable use, liability and dispute resolution; any international arbitration clauses require local counsel review.
- Add effective date, governing jurisdiction, company details, user rights/contact instructions and an accessible deletion flow.
- Remove the visible draft banner and `robots: noindex` only after these checks are completed and the live app actually follows the documents.
